# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful, assume good faith, protect people’s privacy, and focus disagreement on evidence and project decisions.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, coordinated false reports, and attempts to use this project for personal retaliation are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s contact options. Do not place sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially to the extent the platform permits and will disclose conflicts of interest.
